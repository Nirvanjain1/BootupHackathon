Listening and reaction

An audio-first exercise for practising reaction and coordination. Every obstacle announces where it is and what to do through sound, so it works with your eyes closed and headphones on.

Gesture controls: move your hand to the left, centre or right of the camera view to change lane. Show four fingers up to start. In hard mode, little finger only jumps and thumb and little finger out ducks. You can change these with Customize gestures. Keyboard controls also work.

How to play

Sound Meaning Do this Practice
Low buzzing rumble, from the left, middle or right Wall in that lane Switch lane (← →) Key 1
Beeps rising in pitch, centred, plus the spoken word “Jump” Hurdle Jump (↑) Key 2
Tone falling in pitch, centred, plus the spoken word “Duck” Low beam Duck (↓) Key 3
Sparkling shimmer, from a lane Coin Be in that lane Key 4

Practice mode (P) lets you calibrate your ears: obstacles come more slowly, nothing can hurt you, and a soft low thud tells you when you would have been hit. Press Escape to stop. Normal mode has only walls and coins. Hard mode adds hurdles (jump) and low beams (duck). Choose with N or H before starting. Your lane is signalled by beeps: one beep from the left is the left lane, two from the centre is the middle lane, three from the right is the right lane. Louder and higher-pitched means closer. Sounds use 3D spatial audio, so with headphones you can hear walls and coins sweep in from the left or right as they approach. Immersive mode (I) is on by default and positions sounds relative to you: a sound in your own lane is centred, a sound one lane over plays only in that ear, and a sound two lanes over plays in that same ear too but sounds duller, quieter and pulses at half speed. Keys: arrows or A/D/W/S. Touch: swipe left, right, up or down; tap to start. You have three lives.
