Practice mode (P) lets you calibrate your ears: obstacles come more slowly, nothing can hurt you, and a soft low thud tells you when you would have been hit. Press Escape to stop. Normal mode has only walls and coins. Hard mode adds hurdles (jump) and low beams (duck). Choose with N or H before starting. Your lane is signalled by beeps: one beep from the left is the left lane, two from the centre is the middle lane, three from the right is the right lane. Louder and higher-pitched means closer. Immersive mode (I) positions sounds relative to you: a sound in your own lane is centred, one lane over is clearly to that side, and two lanes over is fully to that side and a little quieter. Keys: arrows or A/D/W/S. Touch: swipe left, right, up or down; tap to start. You have three lives.
